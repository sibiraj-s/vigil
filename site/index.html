Vigil — keep your Mac awake

Vigil is a free, open-source menu bar app for macOS. Start a session with one click, or let triggers keep your Mac awake on their own: during a Zoom call, on an external display, while a download finishes.

  • Sessions indefinitely, for 15 minutes to 5 hours, until a time of day, while an app runs, or while a download finishes
  • A countdown next to the menu bar icon for timed sessions
  • Triggers: chosen apps running, an external display or drive, power source, Wi‑Fi network, a weekly schedule
  • Global keyboard shortcuts, a battery guard, and an option to let the display sleep
